Source and limitations

OPeRA is a small research sample from real human shopping sessions (not a market-wide or retailer-wide event log). The product metadata is extracted from product records attached to observed browser actions. The source includes product-link clicks and purchase-intent controls; a purchase-intent click is not evidence that an order completed.

The chronological session split yields too few eligible next-product targets for a reliable accuracy benchmark. Recommendation examples are interactive demonstrations; no Recall/NDCG performance claim is made.
