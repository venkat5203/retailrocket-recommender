Source, methodology and limitations

Product metadata is taken from the products attached to observed browser actions. Product-link clicks and purchase-intent button clicks are separate action types. A purchase-intent click is not evidence that an order was completed. The source is a small research sample from real participants, not a retailer-wide clickstream or market-representative population.

The evaluation uses a session-level chronological 80/20 split. It evaluates the next product-associated item in a held-out session sequence. The results are an offline research demonstration and are not an online A/B test.
